Theoretical Foundations

> The 3-Polarizer "Paradox"

This is historically called a paradox, but it perfectly follows the rules of vector projection. If you cross two polarizers at 0° and 90°, 100% of the light is blocked because a vertical wave has zero horizontal components. However, if you insert a third polarizer between them at 45°, the first vertical wave is forced to project itself diagonally. This new diagonal wave now possesses a horizontal component, allowing exactly 12.5% of the original light to successfully slip through the final 90° filter!

> Fast Axis vs. Transmission Axis

Polarizers have a Transmission Axis. They absorb any electric field vector perpendicular to this line.
Waveplates have a Fast Axis. They are made from birefringent crystals where light traveling along the Fast Axis moves slightly faster than on the orthogonal Slow Axis. Because these axes are physically locked 90° apart inside the crystal lattice, changing the Fast Axis slider automatically rotates the entire physical crystal.
